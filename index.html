How it works

From “we should” to actually going.

01

Plan it.

Say what, where and when. Share it with everyone or only with your friends.

02

Do it.

Tap once to join. You get a group chat with everyone who's going, so you can sort the details and turn up.

03

Remember it.

Afterwards, post your photos and tag the friends who were there. Your Memories tab keeps every Moment you made or joined.

What it fixes

Made to get you out the door.

Plans that die in the group chat

Every Moment has a time, a place and its own chat with just the people who are going.

Photos lost in camera rolls

Photos from a Moment stay with it, tagged with who was there, so you can find them next year.

Time spent scrolling

We count success in hours you spend with people. Time on the app doesn't count.
